Why

Globally, over 485 to 500 bird species and hundreds of millions of wild birds have died or been culled due to the H5N1 highly pathogenic avian influenza panzootic. Over 633 million poultry birds were lost globally to disease or preventative culling between 2005 and 2024, according to the World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH).

The H5 strain of avian influenza threatens wildlife by causing mass mortality events, disrupting ecosystems, and posing a critical risk to endangered species, potentially leading them toward extinction. Around 45% of Australias bird species are found nowhere else in the world. This means Australia has a particularly important responsibility to protect its bird populations Threatened and near threatened Australian bird populations declined by an average of 60% between 1985 and 2020.

The Carnaby's Black - Cockatoo, (a native species to WA) population has been estimated to have declined by around 90% over the past 40 years, with as few as 2,500 birds remaining according to BirdLife Australia. This is part of a worldwide issue affecting bird species: the increasing threat of avian influenza, commonly known as bird flu. Avian influenza is an infectious viral disease caused by influenza A viruses that primarily affects wild and domestic birds.

We had to narrow our focus to how we could help protect native bird species within our own Churchlands community. Thats why we set out to figure out how we could help maintain bird health in the churchlands area, this is how we hope to achieve that.
