Frequently Asked Questions

What is your project?

For our IDL project, Agents of Change, we identified a growing concern for native bird species within the Churchlands community. We wanted to find a practical way to support local birds while also encouraging our community to take an active role in conservation. Our research into avian influenza, commonly known as bird flu, showed us that protecting and supporting healthy native bird populations is an important environmental issue.

What are your goals?

Our goals are to create a healthy environment for native birds in the school. In the process, we aim to integrate different aspects of learning, such as woodworking, art, Indigenous design, nvironmental sciences, and increase the number of native birds in the school while creating a stronger community environment.

What do you aim to do?

We aim to research and recommend native plants that are suitable for the Churchlands environment and provide birds with natural sources of food, shelter and nesting areas. These plants can be grown in school gardens, local community spaces and peoples own backyards. By encouraging the growth of native plants, we can help provide birds with the resources they need without relying on people to feed them directly. We will also encourage community members to make their backyards more welcoming to native birds by planting a variety of suitable native species. This can create small habitats throughout the community, giving birds more opportunities to find food and shelter.

Have you faced any problems in your project?

Some problems we have come across through our IDL are; how do we present our idea into the school and make it a fesaible ciriculium subject. Is this edecuation project even plausable, would it even make the changes we wan't. Further more we spent time assesing if the project was even a realistic way to prevent bird flu, is education really effective in combating issues, or is it a lost cause. These were the broad overview of the chalanges we faced while working on this project.

Whats your solution?

Our solution is simple, Certificate II Conservation students would plant suitable native species within the bushland surrounding Churchlands, while Community Service students would water and maintain the plants every Monday, Wednesday and Friday morning. At the same time, older year Woodworking students would freely design and build birdhouses suited to local native birds. These would then be passed to senior Art students, who could decorate them with respectful Indigenous inspired designs.
